Narro

One OpenRouter key gives you speech models from many providers, such as ElevenLabs, Google, MiniMax, Deepgram and Mistral. The list comes live from OpenRouter.

Kokoro-82M runs entirely on your machine. The first use downloads the model (~90 MB on CPU, ~300 MB with WebGPU); after that it works offline.

Supertonic 2 by Supertone runs on your machine and speaks English, Spanish, French, Portuguese and Korean. The first use downloads the model (~260 MB); after that it works offline. Model licence: OpenRAIL.

Piper voices run on your machine. Each voice downloads once (~60 MB, more for high quality) and then works offline. Voices come from rhasspy/piper-voices; each has its own licence on its model card.

Point at any server exposing /v1/audio/speech, e.g. Kokoro-FastAPI, Orpheus-FastAPI or LocalAI.
