The Mill on the River

A short story · 4 min read

The river bent twice before it reached the old mill. Nobody in the village remembered who had built it, and the records that might have told them burned long ago.

Each spring the water rose and covered the lower steps. Children dared each other to touch the rusted wheel, and their parents warned them, but the warnings never worked.

One summer a stranger arrived with a box of tools. He said he could make the wheel turn again by autumn. Most people laughed, though a few brought him bread.

By September the wheel was turning. Nobody laughed after that.
